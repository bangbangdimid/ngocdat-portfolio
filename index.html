A Note from the Artist

Watercolour on
paper, mostly. A few
ideas in ink.

I keep this place the way one keeps a sea-glass jar — a slow accumulation of small, considered things. Each piece here was made for a reason: a class to teach, a question to answer, a problem to think aloud about.

You'll find lectures, course notes, a few stubborn papers, working code, and the occasional unfinished thought. Take what's useful; sign in if you'd like a copy.

— Linh.
